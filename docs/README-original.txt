MONSTER MASH TRADE HUB
A fan-made sticker tracker for the Monopoly GO Monster Mash album (Demon Hunters collab).

OPEN IT
  1. Unzip everything into one folder.
  2. Double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox).
  3. Keep the "assets" folder next to index.html. It holds the sticker art, tokens, favicons and UI art. Sticker, token, favicon and UI artwork use WebP; platform PWA launcher icons use PNG where required.

YOUR DATA
  Everything you enter is saved in this browser on this device.
  To keep a copy or move to another device: Import & Export > Full backup (.json),
  then drop that file on the Import & Export page on the other device.

GETTING ACCOUNTS IN
  - By hand: Accounts > Add account.
  - From files: Import & Export > drop your account list, friend list and sticker CSVs.
    Accounts are matched by player ID first, then by name. You see a preview of every
    change before anything is saved, and Undo is there right after.

SAMPLE FILES (samples folder)
  sample-monster-mash-album.csv
    Monster Mash sticker data for your accounts, in the same columns as your album export.
    It shows the variations you'll meet: stickers owned or missing, spare counts,
    manual count overrides, alternate sticker names (HUNTRIX, Kings of Swing, Chillings),
    one account with nothing yet, and one with the whole album done.
  sample-accounts.csv / sample-friends.csv
    Your account and friend lists in the same format as your exports.

SOUND
  Sound effects start after your first click (browsers require that).
  Spooky ambience is off until you switch it on in the right-hand menu.
