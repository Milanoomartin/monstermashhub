Monster Mash Trade Hub — Monster Mash Arcade Update

WHAT WAS ADDED
- Dedicated Games page in desktop navigation and mobile More menu.
- 72 playable minigames across 18 different mechanics.
- 12-character arcade cast selector, including Frankie, Disco Drac, Ghostly Guide, Wolfie, Rags, Swampie, Hexie and Skyblade/Demon Hunter characters.
- Five intensity levels: Chill, Easy, Medium, Hard and Nightmare.
- Search, category and intensity filters, favorites, random-game button, play counts and persistent high scores.
- Full touch/mouse controls plus keyboard controls where useful.

ARTWORK INTEGRATION
- Sticker images appear in treasure, memory, breakout, catch, runner, pop and other cabinets.
- Favicons are playable characters and targets.
- Tokens appear in themed covers, racers and related games.
- Trick or Treat buckets are used in catch games.
- Existing jumpscare art/sound can trigger in high-intensity failures.
- Halloween/Monster Mash emoji are used as hazards, markers and targets.

MONOPOLY GO-INSPIRED CABINETS
The arcade includes fan-made haunted variations inspired by mechanics seen in Prize Drop, Treasures/Dig events, Racers, Partner builds, Juggle Jam-style events, Wheel Boost and Adventure Club card challenges. These are unofficial fan minigames, not copies of the live MONOPOLY GO event data or reward economy.
