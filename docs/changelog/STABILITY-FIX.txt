MONSTER MASH TRADE HUB — ARTWORK + AUDIO STABILITY FIX
September 21, 2026

WHY SOME IMAGES COULD TURN INTO BLANK BOXES
The three artwork mapping bundles were loaded with async script tags. That created a timing race: the page could render and mark image placeholders before the asset map containing their paths had arrived. Because the old hydrator considered some image elements finished after its first attempt, a late bundle or a browser sleep/network hiccup could leave a blank box without a retry.

ARTWORK FIXES
- Artwork map scripts now load with defer in deterministic order.
- Every artwork bundle now announces when it has arrived.
- The page repairs any waiting image placeholders when a bundle arrives.
- Failed image elements automatically retry several times.
- Failed decoded Image objects used for canvas/export are discarded and recreated.
- Artwork is checked/repaired when the page returns from the background, restores from browser back/forward cache, or comes back online.
- Existing service-worker image caching remains in place.

WHY SOUND COULD SHOW ON BUT BE SILENT
Web Audio is subject to browser autoplay rules. Firefox and mobile browsers can also suspend an AudioContext after a tab is backgrounded or a device sleeps. The previous build only registered one first-interaction audio wake-up. If the browser suspended audio again later, the setting could remain enabled while the audio engine stayed suspended.

AUDIO FIXES
- Audio resume is attempted on every pointer/touch/keyboard interaction.
- Audio is also checked when the tab becomes visible again or restores from page cache.
- A closed/discarded AudioContext is rebuilt automatically.
- Sound effects wait for a successful AudioContext resume before firing.
- Haunted ambience keeps its saved on/off preference separate from the temporary runtime state.
- The toolbar can show “Haunted ambience · tap to wake” when enabled but temporarily suspended.
- Potion Lab now includes an Audio status readout and a “Wake + test” button.

SERVICE WORKER
Cache version bumped from monster-mash-v4 to monster-mash-v5-stability so the updated application shell replaces the older cached build.
