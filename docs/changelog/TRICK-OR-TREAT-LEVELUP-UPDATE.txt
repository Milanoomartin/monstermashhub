Monster Mash Trade Hub update

Changes in this build:
- Removed the visible Games tab and redirected any saved Games page back to Album.
- Upgraded Trick or Treat into a bucket-catch survival mode.
- Ghosts, witches, spiders, and bats now chase candy; if they reach your bucket they steal treats.
- Tapping/clicking those creatures spins them off the screen.
- Helper buckets unlock at 50, 225, and 500 treats as mirrored catch zones.
- Multi-touch support lets touch users control multiple catch points at once.
- Candy Vortex, Double Treats, and Moon Freeze remain active boosts.
- Tutorial updated to cover the current feature set and remove Arcade references.
