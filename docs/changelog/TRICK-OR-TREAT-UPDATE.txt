Monster Mash Trade Hub — Trick or Treat + Event Roster Update
Build: v11-trick-or-treat

EVENT PLANNER
- Fixed the duplicate Event Planner navigation entry. There is now one desktop Event Planner button and one mobile More-menu entry.
- Event planners can select existing Trade Hub accounts from dropdowns or use Manual / not in account list for outside players.
- Tracked accounts cannot be assigned twice inside the same event plan.
- Current participant caps are enforced by the planner layout:
  • Community Chest: 3 chest partners
  • Racers: 4 racers total, including the planning account
  • Partner Build: 4 partner slots / attractions
  • Adventure Club: 5 players total, including the planning account
- Racers and Adventure Club reserve Player 1 for the planning player when one is selected or manually entered.
- Existing saved event data is normalized so account-link fields are added without wiping progress.

CLICKABLE HAUNTED DECOR
- Click the roaming ghost to summon a full Ghost Parade.
- Click the flying witch to summon a seven-witch Witch Parade with lightning and audio.
- Click a roaming spider to burst a spider swarm across the screen; cobwebs follow shortly afterward.

TRICK OR TREAT MODE
- Added a persistent mystery Trick or Treat bucket in the top-right corner of every page.
- Click the mystery bucket to start or stop Trick or Treat mode.
- On desktop/fine-pointer devices the selected bucket becomes the cursor follower while the hunt is active.
- Candy travels across the viewport and can be clicked/tapped.
- Caught treats animate into the top-corner bucket and increase Treat count, streak and best streak.
- A randomized share of candy is secretly a Trick. Catching one triggers a full-screen jump scare, lightning and a synthesized scream/shrieking sound.
- Trick/Treat totals, streaks and unlocked buckets persist with the rest of the Trade Hub data.

BUCKET COLLECTION
- Collection now has a dedicated Trick or Treat Buckets tab.
- 10 themed starter buckets are available immediately.
- 10 special buckets unlock through Treat milestones and can be equipped as the Trick or Treat cursor bucket.
- Special bucket unlock thresholds: 10, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150, 225, 325, 500 and 750 Treats.
- Unlocks get a dedicated celebration overlay and candy burst.
